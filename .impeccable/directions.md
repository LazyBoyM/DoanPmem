# Grounded direction study

Product mechanism: shared training records connect course registration, teaching, transcripts and resits under three scoped roles. Scene: Vietnamese university users working on laptops in offices/classrooms and phones between classes. The first screen must locate a task and its current academic scope immediately.

Avoided rut: generic teal KPI-card dashboard; its predictable opposite is an ornamental dark analytics cockpit.

1. University wayfinding: white reading field, cobalt institutional signage and a precise sans; a short route-and-scope strip connects every workspace to its academic context. Familiar and practical, with a risk of looking like most institutional apps.
2. Library accession identity: chalk white, plum catalog ink and clear tabular records; searches produce a stable identity-first list. Useful for profiles but less strong on schedule interaction.
3. Printed semester timetables: muted porcelain, blue ink and amber time annotations; a consistent course-and-week ribbon leads the work. Familiar across registration and teaching, without ruled-paper imitation.
4. University journal identity: warm white, forest headings and graphite data; concise section typography and a restrained contents rhythm. Strong reading identity, less immediately distinctive for dense operation.
5. Graduation regalia: navy shell, white content and muted gold milestones; academic completion is expressed as a linear credit measure. Ceremonial origin, translated into standard product controls.
6. Campus courtyard signage: terracotta signs, shaded neutral ground and foliage green status; routes and semester scope anchor pages. Warm everyday context, without depicting architecture.
7. University athletic pennants: wine identity, pale lilac work ground, white data and emerald completed states; course progress is a numbered-by-meaning credit strip rather than ornamental metrics. Strong institution-wide identity, used through palette/type/density and one segmented academic progress signature, while navigation and controls stay conventional.

Seed 516106bd assigned #7. Challenger judgments use audience identification and product clarity. Boarding pass and warm consumer UI are competitive; the first strengthens scanning, the second mobile interaction. Star atlas, coded record sleeves, acetate manuals and type specimens are declined because their strongest readings do not match users' academic tasks. Kept disciplines are explicitly shown in the direction board.
